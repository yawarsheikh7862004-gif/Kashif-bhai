THE STORY · THE PURPOSE

Every bird has a story.

Delhi is often described through its monuments, markets and busy roads. But beyond those familiar sights is another Delhi: wetlands alive with calls, quiet woodland trails and gardens where extraordinary birds live alongside us.

Birding with Kashif Yameen is built around a simple idea: nature is closer than we think. These personal birding experiences invite you to slow down, observe carefully and discover the remarkable diversity of birds across Delhi NCR.

Our vision.

To make birdwatching welcoming, meaningful and accessible, whether you are picking up binoculars for the first time or looking for a particular species to photograph. We want every outing to encourage curiosity and respect for wild habitats.

Our approach.

Personal experiences

Plan around your interests, pace and available time.

Ethical observation

Keep a respectful distance, avoid disturbing nests and let birds behave naturally.

Learning in the field

Explore identification, habitat, calls and observation techniques.
