Terms & Conditions

Enquiries and confirmation

Submitting an enquiry is not a reservation. Dates, route, group size, quote, payment terms and access must be confirmed in writing before booking.

Inclusions

Guided birdwatching and refreshments are included. Meals and accommodation are excluded. Transport, entry fees and equipment arrangements must be clarified in your quote.

Routes and timings

Multiple destinations depend on travel time, opening hours, weather and access. Routes may need to change for safety or conservation.

Wildlife and conduct

Wild birds are unpredictable; sightings and photography opportunities are not guaranteed. Follow guide instructions and local rules. Children must be supervised by their accompanying adult.

Payments and changes

Only pay after receiving agreed booking and cancellation conditions. Contact Kashif promptly if your plans change.
