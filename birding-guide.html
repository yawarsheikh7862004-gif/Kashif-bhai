Birding Do’s & Don’ts

Do

Wear muted colours, walk quietly, keep a respectful distance, follow designated trails and listen to your guide. Carry water and take litter home.

Don’t

Do not feed, chase, handle or disturb birds, nests or chicks. Avoid playback of calls, flash near sensitive birds and entering restricted areas.

What to bring

Binoculars if available, comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, sun protection, water and a charged camera if desired. Confirm equipment availability with Kashif.

Birding ethics

Put bird welfare first. Avoid sharing precise locations of sensitive nests and respect other visitors and local communities.
